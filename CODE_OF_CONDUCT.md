# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Harassment and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may reject contributions or ban people who ignore this.

Report conduct issues privately via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/koupper-jvm/koupper/security/advisories/new) if they involve abuse of the project, or open a maintainer-only discussion.
